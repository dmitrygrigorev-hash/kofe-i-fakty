Coffee and Facts

A daily Russian-language news digest: Israel, Russia, the world and artificial intelligence — short and factual, every morning.

This app is our own internal tool. It uploads our finished episodes to our own YouTube channel “Кофе и Факты”: the video, title, description and thumbnail. It does not access other people’s accounts or viewers’ data.
